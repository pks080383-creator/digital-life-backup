# Launch checklist

## Infrastructure
- [ ] Confirm Node.js, Python, and n8n versions on the actual PC.
- [ ] Start n8n locally and confirm the editor opens.
- [ ] Import both starter workflows.
- [ ] Test both webhooks with dummy data.
- [ ] Confirm workflows are inactive or test-only until approved.
- [ ] Back up n8n data and keep credentials out of Git.

## Service readiness
- [ ] Prepare one before/after Excel demo using synthetic data.
- [ ] Define exact deliverables, turnaround, exclusions, and revision limit.
- [ ] Prepare a short intake checklist for workbook version, desired output, and deadline.
- [ ] Test workbook audit utility and inspect its report.
- [ ] Prepare a sample delivery note and invoice/payment instructions.

## First customer
- [ ] Publish one focused offer on a suitable free organic channel.
- [ ] Invite a small number of relevant prospects to review the demo; follow platform rules.
- [ ] Track every enquiry and response.
- [ ] Quote only after confirming scope.
- [ ] Ask for permission before using any customer result as a case study.

## Go-live approval
Do not enable automated Gmail sending, paid ads, paid services, or automatic delivery until the owner has reviewed the complete workflow and explicitly approved it.
